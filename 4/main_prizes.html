The Main Prizes

These are the main prizes given out to the wrestler who wins the yusho (championship) in the top division:

  • The Emperor's Cup
  • The championship flag
  • Prime Minister's Cup

Once a Year Trophies

These trophies are only handed out during the last basho of the year in November:

  • Nishinippon Shimbun Award
  • Hochi Wrestler of the Year Award
  • Best Makuuchi Newcomer Award
