Iroh

Iroh, commonly refered to as "Uncle Iroh," "General Iroh," or "The Dragon of the West," is a recurring side character in the animated series "Avatar: The Last Airbender." He is a firebending master who serves as Zuko's (his nephew) mentor. Unlike many firebenders, Iroh is usually calm and easy-going, serving as a stark contrast to his brother Fire Lord Ozai, the main antagonist of the series.

Now I'll give 3 reasons for why I like Iroh:

  1. I like how he guides Zuko into doing the right thing. Throughout the series, Iroh constantly tells Zuko that he must choose his own destiny and not blindly follow the orders of his father, Fire Lord Ozai. This advice ultimately influences Zuko in to betraying Ozai and choosing to help Aang, the Avatar, bring balance to the world.
  2. I like how he shares his wisdom with anyone who is willing to hear. After Zuko parts ways with Iroh, Iroh continues to follow him at a distance. During this time he encounters Toph, the blind earthbending girl who is helping Aang but at the time got fed up with Team Avatar and ran away. Iroh has tea with Toph where she complains about Iroh pouring her tea for her, interpreting the act as Iroh viewing her of incapable of taking care of herself. Iroh clairifies that he simply wanted to do it as a kind gesture, and tells her how she reminds him of his own nephew who also never wants his help, explaining how there's nothing wrong with accepting help from those that care about you. Iroh then tells Toph about how he's following his nephew so he can help him when he needs it. Toph realizes how much Iroh cares for his rebelious nephew, and sees that Team Avatar cares about her just like Iroh does about his nephew, leading Toph to return to the team with a better outlook.
  3. I like how open-minded he is. Usually, firebenders are very hot-headed and arrogant, believing that through sheer offense they can get through any obstacle. Iroh, on the other hand, admired the balance of all the 4 elements, and noticed that waterbenders usually stay calm and collected, taking a more defensive approach to their battles. With this insight, Iroh developed a new technique in firebending using this defensive mindset, allowing him to absorb lightning, redirect it through his body, and shoot it out elsewhere. (FYI firebenders can create and shoot lightning out from their fingertips).
